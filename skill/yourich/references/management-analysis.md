# Management Analysis

Management and capital allocation analysis must be grounded in official
evidence: filings, proxy statements when available, earnings releases, or
company investor materials. Do not infer intent from price movement or generic
reputation.

## Management

Use official evidence for leadership changes, compensation context, governance,
strategic priorities, controls, and disclosed execution issues. If the current
filing set does not contain that evidence, mark the claim as
`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.

## Capital Allocation

Separate dividends, repurchases, acquisitions, debt issuance or repayment,
capital expenditures, and reinvestment. Link each claim to filing evidence or
another official source before interpreting quality.
