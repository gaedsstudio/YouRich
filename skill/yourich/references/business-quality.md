# Business Quality

Business quality is structured analysis, not a numeric score.

Review these dimensions only when evidence is present:

- business model
- revenue drivers
- customer structure
- segments
- geography
- recurring versus transactional revenue
- cost structure
- capital intensity
- industry position
- key dependencies

If a dimension is absent from filing evidence, mark it as insufficient evidence.
Do not force a complete business-model map from weak text.

## Moat

There is no automatic moat conclusion. Treat competitive advantage as
`NO_CLEAR_EVIDENCE` until filing evidence or cited primary sources support the
specific mechanism, such as switching costs, network effects, cost advantage,
brand strength, regulation, or scale economics.
